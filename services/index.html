FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a website project take?
Most projects are delivered in 2 to 4 weeks, depending on complexity and how quickly your content is ready. Custom and e-commerce builds may run a little longer and are scoped upfront. Rush delivery isn't available — we'd rather get it right.
Do I need to provide my own content and images?
We can work with your existing materials or start from scratch. Professional copywriting is available as an add-on ($650) for any package. For images, we source high-quality licensed photography or can incorporate your own photos. We'll guide you on what we need before we start.
Will my website rank on Google?
Every site we build is SEO-ready from day one: proper semantic HTML, keyword-mapped meta tags, schema.org markup, fast loading speeds, and mobile optimization. Growth and Enterprise plans include full keyword research and on-page SEO. Rankings grow over time — we set the right foundation so your investment compounds.
What's your payment structure?
We use a simple split: 50% upfront to kick off the project, 50% on final delivery and approval before launch. Retainer plans are billed monthly on the 1st. All prices are fixed — no surprise hourly overruns. We accept Wise, Payoneer, PayPal, and crypto (USDT).
Can I update the website myself after launch?
Yes — it depends on the build. WordPress sites come with a simple admin panel so you can update text, images, and blog posts yourself, and we provide a short handover walkthrough. For custom-coded sites, we handle updates for you — most clients pair these with a monthly retainer plan that covers ongoing updates and maintenance.
Do you work with businesses outside the Philippines?
Absolutely. 100% of our client base is international — primarily the US, UAE, Australia, Canada, and UK. We work fully remotely via email, Zoom, and project management tools. Timezone differences have never been a problem; we schedule around your working hours.
How many rounds of revisions do I get?
Every project includes two full rounds of revisions at the design stage, plus a final polish round before launch. We work collaboratively from the start, so big surprises are rare — and if you need more, we'll always tell you the cost upfront before doing the work.
Do you handle hosting and domain setup?
Yes. We'll recommend reliable, fast hosting and help you set up your domain and professional email. You keep full ownership of every account — we simply handle the technical setup so your site goes live without the headache. Hosting management is also included in our monthly retainer plans.
Who owns the website once it's finished?
You do — completely. Once the project is paid in full, you own 100% of your website, files, domain, and all accounts. There's no lock-in and no proprietary platform holding your site hostage. It's yours to keep, move, or extend however you like.
What if I'm not happy with the result?
We don't consider a project done until you're genuinely happy with it. Because we share designs early and revise collaboratively, you're never surprised at the end. The 50% deposit only kicks off the work — the final 50% isn't due until you've approved the finished site.
